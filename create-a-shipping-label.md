# Create a shipping label

You can buy a label for any order that has a complete address.

## Before you start

Check that the order has a weight. Parcelpine uses the weight and dimensions to get carrier rates, and orders without them can't be quoted.

## Steps

1. Go to **Orders** and open the order.
2. Enter the package weight and dimensions. You can use kilograms and centimetres or pounds and inches.
3. Click **Get rates**. Parcelpine shows every available service, sorted by price.
4. Choose a service. If the parcel is worth more than $100, turn on **Insurance**. Parcelpine insures parcels up to $5,000.
5. Click **Buy label**.
6. Choose the format, **PDF** or **ZPL**, and click **Print**.

[SNIPPET: label-void-warning]

## After you buy the label

Parcelpine emails your customer a tracking link once the carrier scans the parcel. If you need the carrier to collect parcels, [schedule a pickup](05-schedule-a-pickup.md).
